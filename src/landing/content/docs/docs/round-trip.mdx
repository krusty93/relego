Relego takes the passages you underline while reading and sends them back to
you. Kindle recaps arrive on the device; Kobo recaps arrive in your inbox.
Your library lives on your own hardware, with no Relego account or subscription.

This is the whole system: three stages a highlight passes through, in the order
you will set them up. Kindle readers finish on their device; Kobo readers finish
in the inbox they choose. Start at the top if Relego is new to you, or jump to
the stage you are stuck on.

## Before you start

You need three things:

- A **Kindle or Kobo** you can plug into your computer over USB, or a Kindle
  account with eligible highlights in the Kindle Notebook for
  [browser sync](/docs/reference/kindle-extension/). No device handy? Sample
  files ship with the project, see [Import highlights](/docs/import/).
- A machine to run **Docker**: a laptop, a NAS, a home server. Relego is
  distributed as a container.
- An **SMTP relay** to send mail through. Free tiers are plenty. There is a
  demo relay you can use to test without signing up for anything, see
  [Deliver](/docs/deliver/).

Everything else is covered as you go.

## Your first recap

The web interface is the shortest route from your highlights to a recap:

1. Start the server, then open the [web interface](http://localhost:8080).
2. Upload your reader's highlight file in **Import**, or set up Kindle sync in
  **Sync** using the [browser extension](/docs/reference/kindle-extension/).
3. In **Settings**, add your Kindle address or the inbox you use with Kobo, then save.
4. Open **Recaps** and select **Send recap now**.

That is the full first-run path. The command line is available when you want to
automate imports or prefer working in a terminal.
